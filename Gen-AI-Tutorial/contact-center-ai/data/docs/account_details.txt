My Account Details - Complete Guide

Overview
Your account holds all your personal and billing information. You can access it anytime through the mobile app or web portal.

What Information is Stored
- Personal details: Name, address, phone number
- Account number and activation date
- Service plan details (data, calling minutes, SMS)
- Billing preferences and payment method
- Usage history (last 12 months)
- Device information

How to Access Your Account Online
1. Open the web portal at myaccount.telecom.com
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click "My Profile" in the top menu
4. You'll see all your account details

How to Access via Mobile App
1. Download our mobile app from App Store or Google Play
2. Sign in with your credentials
3. Tap the "Account" tab at the bottom
4. View and manage your account details

Viewing Your Account Summary
The account summary shows:
- Account holder name
- Account number (for reference on bills)
- Service address
- Current plan and any add-ons
- Next renewal date
- Account status (active or inactive)

Updating Your Information
You can update:
- Phone number
- Email address
- Mailing address
- Password
- Communication preferences

To update: Go to Account Settings > Edit Profile > Make changes > Save

Important Security Note
Never share your account password with anyone. Our support team will never ask for your password.

Contact Support
If you need help accessing your account, call our support team at 1-800-TELECOM or chat with us online.
