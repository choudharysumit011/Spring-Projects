How to Check Your Balance - Step by Step

Overview
Your balance represents your remaining usage or credit. You can check it anytime through multiple channels.

Checking Balance Online
1. Log in to myaccount.telecom.com
2. On the dashboard, you'll see your balance in the top right corner
3. Click "Balance Details" for a breakdown by service
4. View remaining data, minutes, SMS, international credits

Checking Balance via Mobile App
1. Open the mobile app
2. Tap the "Usage" tab
3. You'll see your current balance for all services
4. Swipe left/right to see different categories

Checking Balance via Automated Phone Call
1. Call our automated system at 1-800-BALANCE (1-800-225-2623)
2. Enter your account number or phone number
3. Listen to your current balance announcement
4. Press options to hear more details

Understanding Your Balance Types
- Data Balance: Measured in GB or MB
- Talk Time Balance: Measured in minutes
- SMS Balance: Measured in number of messages
- International Balance: Special rates for international calls
- Account Credit: Prepaid balance if you use that plan

Important Notes
- Balance resets monthly on your renewal date
- Unused balance does not roll over to next month
- If you exceed your balance, you may incur overage charges
- Check your balance regularly to avoid surprises

Low Balance Warning
If your balance falls below 20%, you'll receive:
- Email notification
- SMS notification
- Push notification in the mobile app

Upgrade or Add More Balance
You can instantly add more:
- Data packs: Starting from 1GB
- International minutes: Available in packs
- SMS packs: For high-volume texting
- Account credit: For pay-as-you-go plans

For help managing your balance, contact support at support@telecom.com
