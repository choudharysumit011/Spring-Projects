Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Account Questions

Q: How do I change my plan?
A: Log in to myaccount.telecom.com, go to "Plans & Add-ons", select your desired plan, and follow the prompts. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle. You can also call support at 1-800-HELP-NOW.

Q: Can I downgrade my plan?
A: Yes, you can downgrade anytime. Downgrade takes effect on next billing cycle. No early termination fee applies. You'll be credited for unused portion of current plan if eligible.

Q: What happens if I go over my data limit?
A: Once you exceed your monthly allowance, you can either purchase additional data or incur overage charges ($15 per 1GB). You'll receive alerts before this happens.

Q: Can I pause my service?
A: Yes, you can pause for up to 3 months without losing your phone number. No charges during pause. To resume, log in to your account or call support.

Q: How do I port my number to another carrier?
A: Request a port-out authorization code from customer support. Provide this code to your new carrier. The process typically takes 24 hours. No fee charged.

Technical Questions

Q: Which phones are compatible with your network?
A: Any unlocked GSM phone or any CDMA phone sold in the US is compatible. Check our device compatibility tool at myaccount.telecom.com/devices.

Q: Do you support international calls?
A: Yes! Rates vary by country. Call +1-800-INTL-CALL for current rates. International plans starting at $10/month are available.

Q: Is WiFi calling available on my plan?
A: WiFi calling is included on most plans. Enable it in Settings > WiFi Calling. You need WiFi connection to use this feature.

Q: What is 5G and do I have access?
A: 5G is our latest network technology offering faster speeds. Check your area coverage and device compatibility at coverage.telecom.com. Most recent phones support 5G.

Service Issues

Q: Why is my internet slow?
A: Slow speeds can be due to: network congestion, weak signal, too many connected devices, or background apps. Try restarting your device, toggling airplane mode, or moving closer to a strong signal area.

Q: What should I do if I have no service?
A: First, toggle airplane mode on/off. Then restart your device. Check coverage map at coverage.telecom.com. Call support if issue persists.

Q: How do I report a network outage?
A: Every issue you report helps us improve. Use the mobile app to report outages with your location. Outage information is available at status.telecom.com.

Billing Questions

Q: When is my bill due?
A: Your bill is due 20 days from the invoice date, shown on your statement.

Q: Can I change my billing date?
A: Yes, contact billing support at 1-800-BILL-NOW to change your billing date.

Q: What is an early termination fee?
A: If you cancel a contract before its end date, an early termination fee applies (up to $200). This doesn't apply if you switch plans within our service.

Q: Do you offer seniors or student discounts?
A: Limited discounts available. Call 1-800-HELP-NOW to discuss options.

Q: Can I request an itemized bill?
A: Yes, request at myaccount.telecom.com or call 1-800-BILL-NOW. Itemized bills show all charges in detail.

Billing Troubleshooting

Q: My bill is higher than expected. Why?
A: Common reasons: overage charges, new add-ons, upgrade to paid features, or special promotional period ending. Review detailed bill view to identify charges.

Q: How do I dispute a charge?
A: Call 1-800-BILL-NOW within 30 days of the charge. Provide specific details about the disputed charge.

General Questions

Q: What is your customer service phone number?
A: Our main support number is 1-800-HELP-NOW (1-800-435-5669). Hours vary by service type.

Q: Do you have 24/7 support?
A: Chat and automated payment support available 24/7. Live support available 8am-10pm EST.

Q: How can I upgrade my device?

Q: Is my data encrypted?
