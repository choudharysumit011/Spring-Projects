Bill Pay and Payment Methods

Overview
Managing your bill and making payments is easy. You have multiple payment options and can set up automatic payments.

Payment Methods Accepted

Credit/Debit Cards
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- Processing time: Immediate (real-time)
- Fees: None for debit cards, credit card processing may apply
- Security: SSL encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant

Bank Account (ACH Direct Debit)
- Supported: Any US bank account
- Processing time: 1-3 business days from submission
- Fees: None
- Setup: Provide account and routing number

PayPal
- Supported through website
- Processing time: 1-2 business days
- Fees: None
- Benefits: Don't share bank details directly

Wire Transfer
- For large payments only ($500+)
- Processing time: 1-2 business days
- Fees: Your bank may charge wire fees
- Contact support for instructions

Mobile Payment Apps
- Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay
- Processing time: Immediate
- Fees: None
- Available through mobile app

How to Pay Online

Step 1: Log In
- Go to myaccount.telecom.com
- Enter your username and password
- Click "Billing" tab

Step 2: Review Your Bill
- Check billing period and amount due
- Verify due date (typically 20 days from bill date)
- Review detailed charges and data usage

Step 3: Make Payment
- Click "Pay Now"
- Select payment method
- Enter payment amount (can be partial or full)
- Review confirmation details
- Submit payment

Step 4: Confirmation
- You'll receive email confirmation immediately
- Your account will be updated within one business day
- Payment history available in "Billing" section

How to Set Up Automatic Payments

Benefits
- Never miss a payment
- Payment on your chosen date each month
- Automatic late fee prevention
- No action required

Setting Up Auto-Pay
1. Log in to myaccount.telecom.com
2. Go to Billing > Payment Settings
3. Click "Set Up Auto-Pay"
4. Select payment method
5. Choose payment date (before due date recommended)
6. Confirm and save

Modifying Auto-Pay
1. Go to Billing > Payment Settings > Auto-Pay
2. Change payment method, amount, or date
3. Update and save
4. Changes effective next billing cycle

Late Payment and Consequences

Due Date
- Shown on your bill statement
- Typically 20 days from invoice date
- Different for different plan types

Late Payment Fee
- $10 - $15 depending on bill amount
- Applied if payment not received by due date
- Appears on next month's bill

Overdue Account Status
- Day 30: Email reminder sent
- Day 45: Service may be restricted
- Day 60: Service may be suspended
- Day 90+: Account referred to collections

Getting off Late Payment List
- Pay full amount owed plus late fees
- Contact support to discuss payment plan options
- Negotiate fee waiver for first-time late payments

Billing Disputes

Reporting Unauthorized Charges
1. Call 1-800-BILL-NOW (1-800-245-5669)
2. Provide account number and dispute details
3. Investigation typically takes 30-60 days
4. Provisional credit while investigating

Understanding Charges
- Check "Detailed Bill" view
- Identify data usage, add-ons, and features used
- Compare with previous month
- Contact support if charges seem unusual

Payment Policies

Returned Payments
- If payment is declined, you'll get notification
- No fee for first return
- Subsequent returns: $15-$25 fee
- Re-submit payment immediately to avoid late fees

Payment Plans
- Available for customers with good payment history
- Up to 12-month plans
- No interest charged
- Contact billing team: billing@telecom.com

Refunds
- Issued to original payment method
- Processing time: 5-10 business days
- For plan cancellations: Prorated within current billing period
- Cannot refund past due amounts

Contact Billing Support
- Phone: 1-800-BILL-NOW (1-800-245-5669)
- Email: billing@telecom.com
- Chat: Available in mobile app
- Hours: 24/7 for automated payment, limited hours for live support
